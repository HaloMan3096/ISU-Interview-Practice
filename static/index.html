Interview Practice Coach

Practise with questions tailored to your résumé and target job, then get STAR-method feedback. This is a coach, not an answer machine: it won’t write your answers for you.

Set up your practice session

The more specific the title, the more specific the questions.

Tip: aim for 1–2 minutes of speaking, roughly 150–300 words.

Feedback

AI feedback may be wrong. Treat it as guidance and trust your own judgment.
